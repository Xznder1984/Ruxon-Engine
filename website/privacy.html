Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Data Collection

The Ruxon Engine, editor, and this website collect no personal data. No telemetry, no analytics, no tracking.

AI Assistant

The optional AI assistant is BYOK (Bring Your Own Key). When used, data is sent directly to your chosen provider. The Ruxon Engine project does not receive or store this data.

Third-Party Services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect server logs. The support page links to SociaBuzz for donations - their privacy policy applies.
